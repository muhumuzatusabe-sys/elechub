1 Log in to Africa's Talking

Go to account.africastalking.com and sign in with the email linked to your {{ username }} app.

2 Go to Billing & Top Up

On your dashboard, click Billing in the left sidebar or click the Top Up button next to your wallet balance.

3 Select Mobile Money / Card

Select Uganda (UGX). Choose MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, or Credit/Debit Card. Enter your phone number and the amount (e.g. 5,000, 10,000, or 20,000 UGX).

4 Approve USSD Push on Phone

A prompt will pop up on your mobile handset. Enter your Mobile Money PIN to authorize payment. The credit reflects instantly!

Important Note for Personal Gmail Accounts:

Google blocks standard account passwords for third-party SMTP. To use Gmail:

  1. Ensure 2-Step Verification is enabled on your Google account.
  2. Visit myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Create an app named ELECHUB and copy the generated 16-character App Password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
  4. Paste this 16-character code into the Mail Password field below.
